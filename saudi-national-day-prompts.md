# Saudi National Day - 4-Shot Vertical Film Prompts

## Settings (all shots)
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 (1080x1920), 24fps
- Duration: 5 seconds per shot (20 seconds total)
- Tested models: `seedance_2_5` (Plus plan or higher, 480p draft mode available), `gemini_omni` (Basic plan or higher, 15 credits per shot)
- Transitions (light flare / particle sweep) are added in post when joining the shots.

## Shot 1 - Opening
Slow cinematic aerial push-in over the Riyadh skyline at golden hour, Kingdom Centre tower centered and prominent, soft emerald green and warm gold volumetric light rays through haze, floating gold dust particles in the foreground, ends on a soft warm light flare. Photorealistic, cinematic grade, HDR, shallow depth of field, deep emerald green, pure white and warm gold palette, 8K detail, no text, no letters, no logos, no subtitles, uplifting mood.

## Shot 2 - Heritage
Smooth steady dolly glide through the mud-brick alleys of historic At-Turaif, Diriyah, warm low sunlight, green flags gently waving from the rooftops, fine dust floating in sunbeams, opening with a golden light sweep. Photorealistic, cinematic grade, HDR, shallow depth of field, deep emerald green, pure white and warm gold palette, 8K detail, no text, no letters, no logos, no subtitles, uplifting mood.

## Shot 3 - Nature and Land
Sweeping drone shot over red desert dunes at sunset, an Arabian horse galloping along the dune crest in slow motion with a cinematic dust trail behind it, camera tracking alongside and slightly above. Photorealistic, anatomically correct horse, cinematic grade, HDR, shallow depth of field, warm gold light, no text, no letters, no logos, no subtitles, uplifting mood.

## Shot 4 - Celebration
Night sky over a city filled with green and white fireworks, a large green Saudi flag waving in slow motion in the foreground, correctly oriented and not mirrored, clean white sword emblem, soft silhouettes of joyful crowds below. Photorealistic, cinematic grade, HDR, shallow depth of field, deep emerald green, pure white and warm gold palette, no extra text, no logos, no subtitles, no distorted faces, uplifting mood.

## Notes
- Review shot 4 closely: video models can garble the Arabic script on the flag.
- If a tool suggests a preset instead of generating (for example "IN THE DARK"), decline it so your prompt is used.
